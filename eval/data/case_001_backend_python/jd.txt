Senior Backend Engineer
Northwind Logistics, Remote (US)

About the role
We are growing our platform team and looking for a senior backend engineer to
own the services behind our shipment tracking product. You will work with the
rest of the team to design APIs, improve reliability, and mentor two junior
engineers.

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong Python, including async frameworks such as FastAPI or Starlette
- Production experience with PostgreSQL, including query tuning
- Experience building and consuming REST APIs
- Comfortable with Docker and container based deployment
- Solid understanding of automated testing and CI

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with Kubernetes in production
- Familiarity with Kafka or another message queue
- Exposure to Terraform or similar infrastructure as code tooling
- Knowledge of Redis for caching and rate limiting
- Go experience is a plus

What we offer
Competitive salary, equity, and a genuine remote first culture.
